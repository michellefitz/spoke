Talking

You talk, it sorts

No forms, no fields, no picking a priority for every little thing. Tap the mic and say the lot, out of order and half-formed. It reads it back so you know it got it.

Your week

Everything on one screen

Your appointments come from the calendar you already keep. Your to-dos sit right beside them. No more checking two places to find out what today actually looks like.

Made by

One person, for her own list

I built Spoke because my to-do list lived in my head and never shut up. Everything in it is there because I needed it on a Tuesday morning with my hands full.

No fake deadlines

Most apps make you pick a day for everything, then turn it red when you miss a date you never really meant.

Spoke lets things be loose. The dentist has a time because it has a time. Booking the MOT just needs doing this week, so that's what it says. Nothing goes red for missing a deadline you invented to get past a date picker.

Honest bit

What it doesn't do

It doesn't run your family, coach you, or score your week. It won't guess a time for something you didn't give one. It's new, so it gets things wrong sometimes — when it does, Settings keeps a record of everything you said and what it made of it, so you can see exactly where it went sideways.
