# Spoke — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 12 August 2026**

Spoke is made by one person. This policy is written plainly because you should
be able to tell what happens to your words without a lawyer.

## The short version

- There's no account. You never give Spoke your name, email or phone number.
- Your tasks and your calendar stay on your iPhone.
- What you say is sent away to be turned into text and then into tasks. That's
  the one thing that leaves your phone, and it's explained in full below.
- Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build a profile of you.
- There's no tracking, no analytics, no advertising SDK. None.

## What leaves your phone, and why

**Your voice, while you're recording.** When you tap the mic, audio streams to
**Deepgram**, a speech-to-text service, which sends back a transcript. This is
live — the audio is transcribed as you speak and isn't kept by Spoke.

**The transcript, once you stop.** The text of what you said is sent to
**Anthropic** (the makers of Claude) to be turned into tasks — titles,
subtasks, days. The reply comes back and becomes the list you see.

Both requests pass through a small server run for Spoke, which exists so the
service keys aren't hidden inside the app where they could be extracted. It
passes your words through and does not store them.

That's the complete list. Nothing else is transmitted.

## What stays on your phone

- **Your tasks**, including everything you've completed. They live in Spoke's
  own storage on the device and are shared only with Spoke's home-screen
  widget.
- **Your calendar.** If you turn calendar on, Spoke reads your events with iOS
  Calendar permission so it can show them beside your tasks. Those events are
  read on the device and displayed on the device. They are never sent to
  Deepgram, to Anthropic, to Spoke's server, or anywhere else.
- **Your recording history.** Settings → Recordings keeps the last 100
  recordings — what you said and what Spoke made of it — so you can check what
  happened when something comes out wrong. It's stored on the device, it isn't
  transmitted, and you can wipe it at any time with "Clear history".

## Permissions Spoke asks for

- **Microphone** — required. It's how you use the app.
- **Calendar** — optional. Say no and everything else works; you just won't see
  appointments in the week view. You can change your mind in iOS Settings →
  Privacy & Security.

## Who else is involved

Two companies process your words on Spoke's behalf:

- **Deepgram** — speech-to-text. https://deepgram.com/privacy
- **Anthropic** — turns the transcript into tasks. https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Their own policies cover what they do with data sent to them. Spoke sends them
only what's described above: audio while recording, and the resulting text.

## Data you can delete

Because there's no account, there's nothing of yours held on a server to
delete. Deleting the app removes your tasks and your recording history from the
device. "Clear history" in Settings → Recordings clears the history on its own
and leaves your tasks alone.

## Children

Spoke isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change ever
means your words go somewhere new, that will be said plainly in the app, not
buried here.
